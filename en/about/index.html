About CROT

We're the crew that pulls pipes under the road so nobody has to break it open.

What we do

CROT carries out horizontal directional drilling (HDD): we install water, sewer and gas pipes, power cables and fibre under roads, pavements, fences and foundations, without trenches. Our motto is simple: Fast. Clean. No digging.

How we approach the work

Respect for what’s on top. The client’s road, yard and fence should stay as they were. That’s why we work from small pits at the ends of the route and don’t open trenches.

Precision, not luck. Every route is steered from the surface along its whole length. We know where the drill head is at every moment and come out exactly at the agreed point.

Straight talk. We tell you from the start whether the job can be done trenchless and what it involves. No promises we can’t keep.

Who we work for

  • Homeowners who want water, gas, sewer or power in the yard without digging it up.
  • Construction companies that need fast, clean connections on site.
  • Network operators who have to cross roads without stopping traffic.
