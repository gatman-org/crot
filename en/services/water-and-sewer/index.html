Trenchless water and sewer connections

We bring water and sewer to the house or building without tearing up the road, the yard or the garden.

When you need HDD for water and sewer

The mains are on the other side of the road, the yard is already paved, or the lawn has only just taken root. In all of these cases a trench means damage, reinstatement and lost time. With horizontal directional drilling the pipe passes under the obstacle and nothing changes on top.

What we do

  • Water connections from the street main into the yard or up to the house.
  • Sewer connections to the public network, under the road or pavement.
  • Routes under landscaped yards: paving, lawns, paths, parking areas.
  • Protective ducts that the pipe can be pulled through later.

Who it’s for

Homeowners avoid a dug-up yard and the bill for relaying the paving. Construction companies get their connections quickly without blocking site access. Network operators can make service connections without closing the street.

How the job runs

We fix the route and the entry and exit points, drill a guided pilot bore, enlarge it to the pipe diameter and pull the pipe through in one length. Afterwards only two small pits remain, and they are backfilled straight away.

How we work: 3 stages

A clear process where every stage is visible and controlled.

  1. 01

    Setting the underground route

    We plan the pipe's path under the obstacle and drill a pilot bore, steered from the surface.

  2. 02

    Sizing the bore for the pipe

    We enlarge the drilled channel to the right diameter for the pipe or protective duct.

  3. 03

    Precise final installation

    We pull the pipe through the prepared channel and leave it ready to connect at both ends.
